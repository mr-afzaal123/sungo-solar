Questions about our products

More questions? See all FAQs or ask us directly.

Are these products in stock?

Yes. Everything on this page is ready stock at our shop in Model Town, Gujranwala. Stock can sell out quickly, so please confirm on WhatsApp or phone before you visit.

Why are prices not shown on the website?

Solar prices in Pakistan change often, sometimes every week. To make sure you get the correct price, tap "Ask price" on any product and we will send you today's price on WhatsApp.

Can you install the products I buy?

Yes. Our technical team installs complete solar systems in Gujranwala and in other cities of Punjab. You can buy products only, or buy and get installation from us.

Which panel, inverter and battery should I choose?

It depends on your load and budget. Send us a photo of your electricity bill and a list of what you want to run, and we will suggest a matching panel, inverter and battery for you.
