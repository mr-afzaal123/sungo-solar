Which inverter brands do you sell?

We are an official dealer of Knox, Itel and Photon inverters. We also supply Fronus, GoodWe, MaxPower, NS, Sofar and Solis. Items marked Ready stock are at our Gujranwala shop now; others can be ordered for you.

Which inverter size do I need?

For fans, lights and a fridge, a 3kW to 4kW inverter is usually enough. To run one 1.5 ton inverter AC, most homes choose 6kW. For two ACs or a bigger house, choose 8kW to 12kW. Send us your bill on WhatsApp and we will suggest the right size.

What is the difference between hybrid and on-grid inverters?

A hybrid inverter works with batteries, so you keep power during load-shedding. An on-grid inverter has no battery backup and is used mainly to cut the bill with net metering.

What does IP54, IP65 or IP66 mean?

It shows how well the inverter is protected from dust and water. IP21 inverters must stay indoors. IP54 and higher can handle more dust, and IP65 or IP66 inverters can be installed outdoors.
