What size solar system do I need for my home?

It depends on what you want to run. As a simple guide, 3 kW runs lights, fans, a fridge and a TV. 5 kW can also run one 1.5 ton inverter AC in the daytime. 8 to 10 kW suits a bigger home with two or more ACs. Send us your electricity bill on WhatsApp and we will tell you the right size.

Can solar run my AC?

Yes. A 5 kW system can usually run one 1.5 ton inverter AC during the day when there is good sunlight. For two ACs, you normally need 8 kW or more. To run an AC at night, you also need a battery. We check your appliances before we suggest a system.

Will solar work at night and during load-shedding?

Solar panels only make electricity in daylight. For power at night or during load-shedding, you need a hybrid inverter and a battery. The battery charges in the day and gives power when the sun is down or the grid goes off. An on-grid system without a battery switches off when the grid goes off.

What is the difference between on-grid, off-grid and hybrid solar?

On-grid solar works with the grid (WAPDA/GEPCO) and can send extra power to it, but it has no backup. Off-grid solar uses batteries and does not connect to the grid at all. Hybrid solar does both: it works with the grid and uses a battery for backup. Many homes in Punjab choose hybrid.

Which battery is better, lithium or the old type?

Lithium batteries last longer, charge faster and need no water or maintenance. Old lead-acid (tubular) batteries cost less at the start, but they need care and usually wear out sooner. We sell lithium batteries from Knox, Itel, Photon, Pylontech and other brands.

Can you help with net metering?

Yes. We help you with the net metering process so you can send extra solar electricity to the grid and lower your bill. Net metering rules in Pakistan can change, so we explain the current rules to you before you apply.
