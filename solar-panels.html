Solar Panel Questions

More questions? See all FAQs or ask us on WhatsApp.

Which solar panel brands do you sell?

We sell solar panels from Aiko, Astronergy, Canadian Solar, JA Solar, Jesko, Jinko, Longi and Risen. All panels on this page are available at our shop in Model Town, Gujranwala.

Which panel wattage should I choose?

Higher-wattage panels (like 620W to 740W) make more power per panel, so you need fewer panels and less roof space. We suggest the right panel after checking your roof and your electricity bill.

What do HJT and bifacial mean?

HJT panels use a newer cell technology that works well in heat. Bifacial panels can also collect light from the back side, which gives extra power when they are mounted with space underneath.

Do you install the panels as well?

Yes. Our technical team installs panels together with the inverter and battery in Gujranwala and across Punjab. Our shop is only in Gujranwala, and the team travels to you.
