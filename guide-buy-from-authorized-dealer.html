How to Make Sure Your Solar Products Are Genuine

By the SunGo Solar team, Gujranwala · 25 September 2026

Quick answer

Buy from an official dealer, ask for a proper invoice and warranty card, check the serial number on the product, and be careful when a price is much lower than the market. Genuine products come with warranty support when something goes wrong.

Why It Matters

Solar equipment is a big investment and should work for many years. Fake or grey-market products can fail early, and the warranty may not be honoured. A few simple checks protect your money.

5 Simple Checks Before You Buy

  1. Buy from an official dealer. Ask the shop which brands they are officially authorized for. SunGo Solar is an official dealer of Knox, Itel and Photon.
  2. Get a proper invoice. It should show the shop name, product model and date. You need it for warranty claims.
  3. Ask for the warranty card. Check that the serial number on the card matches the serial number on the product.
  4. Check the model and specs. Make sure the wattage, kW or voltage on the label matches what you paid for.
  5. Be careful with very low prices. If a price is far below other shops, ask why. It may be old stock, a different model or not genuine.

What If Something Goes Wrong?

When you buy from us, we help you with warranty claims for the products we sell and install. Keep your invoice and warranty card safe, and call our technical team if there is a problem.
