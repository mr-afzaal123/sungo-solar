Lithium Battery Questions

More questions? See all FAQs or ask us on WhatsApp.

Which lithium battery brands do you sell?

We are an official dealer of Knox, itel Energy and Photon batteries. We also supply Crown, Dyness, Fronus, Hisel, Inverex, Pylontech, Pytes, Royal, Sofar, Solis, Soluna and Volnex. Items marked Ready stock are at our Gujranwala shop now; others can be ordered for you.

Which battery size do I need?

A 2.5kWh battery suits a small home with fans and lights at night. A 5kWh battery is the most popular size for homes. For an AC at night or a bigger house, choose 10kWh to 16kWh. Send us your bill on WhatsApp and we will suggest the right size.

What is the difference between 24V and 48V (51.2V) batteries?

The battery voltage must match your inverter. Small inverters usually use 24V batteries, and most 4kW and bigger inverters use 48V (51.2V) batteries. We check your inverter before suggesting a battery.

Are lithium batteries better than tubular batteries?

Yes, for most homes. Lithium batteries last much longer, charge faster and need no water or maintenance. Tubular batteries cost less at the start but wear out sooner.

Do you install lithium batteries?

Yes. Our technical team installs and sets up batteries with your inverter in Gujranwala and across Punjab. Our shop is only in Gujranwala, and the team travels to you.
