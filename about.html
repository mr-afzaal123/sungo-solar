About SunGo Solar

SunGo Solar is a solar energy company in Model Town, Gujranwala. Since 2022 we have sold and installed more than 500 solar systems for homes, shops, factories and farms across Punjab.

Who We Are

We are a local solar shop that does the full job: we help you choose the right system, we supply genuine products, our own team installs them, and we stay with you after installation.

We are an official dealer of Knox, itel Energy and Photon, and we also sell trusted brands like Longi, Jinko, Canadian Solar, JA Solar, Solis and GoodWe. You will also find us at solar industry events and exhibitions in Punjab.

About SunGo Solar: Quick Answers

More questions? See all FAQs or ask us on WhatsApp.

Who owns SunGo Solar?

SunGo Solar was founded by Javed Akram, who is the Founder and CEO. Zahid Akram leads the technical team, and Shahbaz Akram leads sales support.

When did SunGo Solar start?

SunGo Solar started in 2022 in Gujranwala. Since then, we have installed more than 500 solar systems in Gujranwala and other cities of Punjab.

Does SunGo Solar have branches in other cities?

No. SunGo Solar has one shop only, in Model Town, Gujranwala. Our team travels to other cities in Punjab to install solar systems.
