Installation questions

More questions? See all FAQs or ask us on WhatsApp.

Do you install solar in my city?

Our shop is in Gujranwala, and our team travels to install solar in Lahore, Sialkot, Wazirabad, Gujrat, Faisalabad and other cities of Punjab. Send us your location on WhatsApp and we will confirm.

Do I need a battery with my solar system?

You need a battery only if you want power at night or during load-shedding. If you mainly want to cut your daytime bill, an on-grid system without a battery can be enough. We will explain both options.

Can you help with net metering?

Yes. We guide you through the net metering process so you can send extra solar electricity to the grid. Net metering rules in Pakistan can change, so we explain the current rules before you apply.

What happens after installation?

We test the whole system before we leave. After that, you can call us any time for checkups, repairs or help with warranty claims.
