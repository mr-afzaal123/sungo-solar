Solar Panel Verification

Check if your solar panel is original before it goes on your roof. Find the serial number on the panel label, then check it on the panel maker’s official website using the links below.

Find your brand Ask us to check

How to Verify a Solar Panel Online

  1. 1

    Find the serial number

    Look at the label on the back of the panel. Write down the serial number or barcode exactly, or take a clear photo of it.

  2. 2

    Open the brand’s official checker

    Find your panel’s brand below and tap its Verify button. It opens the maker’s own website.

  3. 3

    Enter the number and compare

    Type the serial number and submit. Check that the model and wattage shown match your panel and your invoice.

Other Ways to Spot a Fake Solar Panel

  • Printed serial number. On a genuine panel the serial number is printed or laser-marked, not a loose sticker that peels off easily.
  • Model and wattage match. What the verification website shows should match the label and your invoice.
  • Look closely. Check for cracked cells, uneven lines, a bent frame, bubbles or loose edges.
  • Warranty on paper. Ask for the warranty on your invoice and know who handles claims in Pakistan.
  • Too cheap is a warning. A branded panel priced far below the market may be fake or a factory reject.
  • Buy from a known shop. Keep your bill. It is your proof when you claim warranty.

Verification Questions

More questions? See all FAQs or ask us on WhatsApp.

Where is the serial number on a solar panel?

On the label (nameplate) on the back of the panel. Many panels also show it on the front top, under the glass, or on the side of the frame. Some newer panels have a QR code next to it.

The website says my serial number is not found. Is the panel fake?

Not always. Check that you typed every letter and number correctly, and try again later because some sites are slow. If it still is not found, contact the panel maker using the details on their page, or send us a photo of the label on WhatsApp and we will help you check.

Does a valid serial number prove the panel is good?

It shows the serial number is real and made by that brand. It does not test the panel itself. Also check that the model and wattage on the website match the label, the panel looks new with no cracks or damage, and the warranty is written on your invoice.
