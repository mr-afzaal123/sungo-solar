Which brands is SunGo Solar an official dealer of?

SunGo Solar is an official dealer of Knox, Itel and Photon. We also sell many other brands, including Longi, Jinko, Canadian Solar, JA Solar, Solis, GoodWe and Pylontech.

Can I mix brands in one solar system?

Yes. Many systems use panels from one brand, an inverter from another and a battery from a third. We check that they work well together before we suggest them to you.
